# Project form (Hello FOSS)

**Project name:** openbeam

**Short description:** Learn beam loading and deflection: shear, moment, deflection and the lightest beam that passes, in 3 sessions.

**Full description:** openbeam is a beginner-friendly learning repo about beams, the horizontal members that carry floors, bridges and shelves. In three short sessions you go from the reactions, shear force and bending moment of a single point load, to the second moment of area, deflection and bending stress with a design problem (the lightest section that meets a deflection limit), to a small finite-element solver that you validate against exact formulas, refine on meshes and use to check a footbridge-style beam. Every session follows the same six steps (real-world problem, theory, visual explanation, interactive simulation, worked example, assessment), and has a lesson script, a Colab notebook, a live slider app and a checkpoint that prints PASS or FAIL. All models are simplified classroom models (straight elastic beams, small deflections); they are never design tools for real structures. The repo is intentionally incomplete: a Target animation and a roadmap show where the community can take it next, such as moving-load envelopes, continuous beams and shear deformation.

**Technologies (comma-separated):** Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, JavaScript, LaTeX
